# Alberta Electricity Market Analysis | Python & Power BI

**Electricity Demand, Pool Price Trends & Market Insights (2020–2025)**

## Project Overview

This project explores electricity demand and wholesale electricity pool prices in Alberta, Canada, using publicly available data from the Alberta Electric System Operator (AESO).

The objective is to identify historical demand patterns, analyze electricity price fluctuations, examine the relationship between demand and market prices, and communicate findings through a four-page Power BI dashboard.

**Analysis period:** January 2020 – July 2025 (2025 is a partial year).

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python & Pandas:** Data preparation, time-based analysis, and statistical calculations
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- **DAX:** Analytical measures and KPI calculations
- **Excel:** Data review and validation

## Dashboard Analysis

The Power BI report contains four analytical pages:

1. **Electricity Market Overview:** Historical electricity demand and pool price trends.
2. **Market KPI Overview:** Summary of key demand and pricing indicators.
3. **Demand & Price Analysis:** Comparison of electricity demand patterns and pool prices.
4. **Annual Correlation Analysis:** Year-by-year examination of the relationship between demand and prices.

## Key Findings

- Alberta electricity demand and pool prices exhibit different patterns over time.
- Pool prices can fluctuate significantly, including periods of very high prices.
- The overall Pearson correlation between electricity demand and pool prices is approximately **0.22**, indicating a weak positive linear relationship.
- Annual correlations vary, suggesting that electricity demand alone does not fully explain market price movements.
- The 2025 data covers only part of the year and should not be interpreted as a complete annual result.
